# HXARK

Open-source tools from [hxark.com](https://hxark.com).

## HXARK Sniffer

**[WoW Retail packet capture for Windows](https://github.com/HXARK/HXARK-Sniffer)**

Open the sniffer, wait for **Ready**, then open WoW and play. Your PKT files save
automatically when you close the game.

- Automatic network interface detection.
- Local capture and validated PKT output.
- No HXARK account required. Captures stay on your PC.
- Open source under the MIT license.

[Download](https://github.com/HXARK/HXARK-Sniffer/releases) ·
[Source and compatibility](https://github.com/HXARK/HXARK-Sniffer#readme) ·
[Report an issue](https://github.com/HXARK/HXARK-Sniffer/issues)

## Contribute

Bug reports and reproducible contributions are welcome. Include the sniffer
version, WoW build and steps to reproduce the issue. Keep credentials, session
keys and private captures out of public issues.
